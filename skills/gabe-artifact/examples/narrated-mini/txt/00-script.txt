A narrated page is built from one script per section. Every paragraph you hear is also written on the page, in the same order, so reading and listening never drift apart.
Each idea arrives in [[n:3|three]] parts. [[parts:1]]A paragraph says it. [[parts:2]]A figure under the paragraph shows it, and lights up while the paragraph is spoken. [[parts:3]]And a cue word points at one piece of the figure, at the moment the voice names that piece.
@fig parts
Nothing is typed twice. The builder takes the scripts, the figures and the recorded voice, and writes one file that travels on its own.
